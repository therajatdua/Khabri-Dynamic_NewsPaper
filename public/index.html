      Questions, answered

      How Khabri works, where the news comes from, and what happens to your data.

      Where does Khabri get its news?

      Straight from the publishers’ own public feeds: The Hindu, The Indian Express, NDTV, The Times of India, Mint and Gadgets 360 for India; BBC, The Guardian, Al Jazeera and NPR for world news; BBC Hindi, NDTV India and Amar Ujala in Hindi. We refresh them every 10 minutes and every story links to the original article.

      How does the For You feed work? Is my data shared?

      You pick topics and follow keywords, and the feed also learns from what you open, save or hide. All of that is stored only in this browser. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking cookies, and nothing about your reading is sent to us.

      Are the AI summaries accurate?

      They’re written by an AI model using only the headline and description the publisher provides, and they’re usually right. AI can still make mistakes, so for anything important read the full story at the source. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact button.

      Can I read the news in Hindi?

      Yes. Open Personalize and set Language to हिन्दी. Your feed, the daily briefing and the AI summaries switch to Hindi sources.

      Can I install Khabri like an app?

      Yes. In Chrome or Edge, use “Install app” in the address bar. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The last feed you loaded stays readable offline.

      How do I reset or delete my data?

      Open Personalize and choose “Reset personalization” to clear topics, follows and reading history. Saved stories can be cleared from the Saved tab. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything.
